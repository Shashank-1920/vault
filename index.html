📲 Install AegisVault App

Your browser supports 1-tap installation directly to your home screen or desktop:

📱 On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow up) at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add in the top right. AegisVault will install as a standalone app!

🤖 On Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge):

  1. Tap the Three Dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm install to add AegisVault to your app drawer and home screen.

💻 On PC / Mac (Chrome / Edge / Brave):

  1. Look at the right side of the address bar at the top of the browser.
  2. Click the Install App icon (⊕ or computer icon).
  3. Or click the 3 dots menu -> Save and share -> Install AegisVault.
